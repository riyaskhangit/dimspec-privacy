Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

DimSpec is a gasket dimension reference and calculation app. This policy explains what information the app stores and how app features handle it.

Information stored on your device

DimSpec stores app preferences and information you create, such as saved size lists, custom dimensions, material selections, and chart edits, locally on your device. DimSpec does not operate an account system or a server that receives this information.

Your local app data remains on your device until you remove it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall DimSpec. Device backups are controlled by your device platform and its settings.

Backups, sharing, and printing

If you choose to export a backup, share a file, or print a chart, DimSpec passes the file to the destination or system feature you select. Those services may handle the file under their own privacy policies. DimSpec does not receive a copy through its own server.

Backup files include a SHA-256 checksum to detect accidental changes. This checksum verifies file integrity; it does not encrypt the backup. Store exported files somewhere appropriate for the information they contain.

Fonts and network requests

DimSpec uses the Google Fonts Flutter library for some interface typefaces. If a font is fetched at runtime, the request goes to Google's font delivery service, which may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. DimSpec does not send your gasket entries or saved lists in that font request.

Tracking and advertising

DimSpec does not include advertising, account-based tracking, or an analytics service in its chart app.

Children

DimSpec is a technical reference tool and is not designed to collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data handling changes. The date above will show when the latest version took effect.
